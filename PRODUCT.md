# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React + Vite, chosen because the user requested a modern runnable frontend and a single-page marketing landing page with lightweight interactions.

## Users

Primary users are owners, marketers, and operations leads at dental clinics and aesthetic medicine institutions in mainland China. They are evaluating how to appear in AI answer engines such as Doubao, Kimi, and DeepSeek when patients ask treatment, price, safety, comparison, and local-provider questions.

## Product Purpose

The landing page sells a service whose core business outcome is helping dental hospitals and aesthetic medicine institutions enter the first few AI search results or recommended candidates in mainland China. Success means a qualified institution understands the ranking opportunity in Doubao, Kimi, and DeepSeek and starts a WeChat consultation.

## Positioning

The page positions the service as a mainland China AI search front-rank growth system focused on getting institutions into the first few AI-recommended candidates. Content, FAQ, entity signals, and evidence structure are delivery methods that support ranking and recommendation placement, not the primary product outcome.

## Operating Context

The service language must fit mainland China search and AI discovery behavior. AI model references should use Doubao, Kimi, and DeepSeek. Contact and CTA flows should use WeChat only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first version must be responsive, directly runnable locally, and include Hero, pain points, AI search trends, SEO versus AI search comparison, dental and aesthetic industry analysis, services, advantages, process, cases or feedback, FAQ, multiple CTAs, and footer.

The commercial offer should include a clearly presented annual paid subscription priced at RMB 100,000 per year, with concrete deliverables for AI front-rank monitoring, ranking opportunity analysis, placement work, conversion handoff, and ongoing iteration.

Do not copy protected complete copy or unique assets from the reference site. Do not fabricate real customer identities or verifiable customer claims; use clearly labeled anonymous or illustrative examples.

## Brand Commitments

The requested visual direction is a modern Chinese marketing page with light blue-gray backgrounds, deep blue headings, fluorescent yellow-green emphasis, rounded cards, medium information density, and refined landing-page pacing.

## Product Principles

- Explain the AI-search ranking shift in business language before asking for contact.
- Make dental and aesthetic use cases concrete enough for an operator to recognize their own patient journey.
- Make front-rank placement, candidate-list inclusion, and ranking tracking the dominant business language.
- Convert through WeChat with low friction and no overseas messenger references.
- Label synthetic, anonymous, and illustrative proof honestly.
- Keep the page polished, credible, and mobile-ready.
